Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ScamLens.

ScamLens is a scam detection tool that analyzes suspicious messages, emails, and URLs to identify common scam patterns and estimate their risk level.

ScamLens checks the input for patterns commonly associated with scams, such as urgency, suspicious links, OTP requests, payment requests, fake rewards, account verification requests, and job-related fees.

Low Risk means few suspicious patterns were detected. Medium Risk means some potentially suspicious patterns were found. High Risk means multiple strong scam indicators were detected.
